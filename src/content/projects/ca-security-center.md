## The challenge

Security research produces different kinds of information: a signal to investigate, source code to examine, or a report that needs validation. Making that work useful depends on how the information is organized and how it supports an investigation.

## My approach

CA Security Center is part of my current security project work. It sits alongside my work on threat-intelligence and static source-analysis platforms, bringing those interests into a more deliberate research workflow.

I approach the work through the questions an analyst needs to answer: what the signal describes, which part of a system it concerns, and what further investigation would establish its significance.

## My contribution

I work on the security tooling and workflows in my own environment. My earlier experience validating exploit reports and analyzing blockchain attack surfaces informs the way I assess the information these tools make available.

## Current scope

The project is active and continues to evolve with my security research. I focus on making research findings useful engineering context, with an investigation process grounded in the system being examined.
