My blockchain work also includes NFT deployment projects. This sits alongside my experience with node infrastructure, enterprise ledger applications, and smart-contract attack-surface analysis.

## My approach

I consider deployment in the context of the contract and the application around it. My security background shapes the questions I ask about minting, permissions, and the behavior expected from a deployed system.

My infrastructure experience adds another perspective: understanding the node and data interfaces that a blockchain application depends on and how those interfaces are used during development and operation.

## My contribution
